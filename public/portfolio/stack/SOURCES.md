## Dark backgrounds

- Use `aws-dark.svg` (Devicon orange variant) and `mqtt-dark.svg` (MQTT solid square with white symbol).
- For `linux.svg`, `mcp.svg`, `ollama.svg`, and `tailscale.svg`, invert the black artwork on dark backgrounds. Linux uses the compact upstream monochrome variant; the multicolor original is 193,922 bytes.
- Render `ultralytics.svg` in white on dark backgrounds, per the [Ultralytics brand guidelines](https://www.ultralytics.com/brand).
